Objective 1 · Task 1

Player discipline

On average, do midfielders pick up more yellow cards per appearance than defenders?

1

Wrangle

Keep only players with ≥1 appearance. Derive cards_per_app = yellow cards ÷ appearances so a seven-match finalist is compared fairly with a one-match substitute.

2

Sample

Population: 319 midfielders and 345 defenders. Simple random sample, without replacement, of n = 45 per position (seed 42).

3

Test

H₀: μMF = μDF. Two-sample t-test at α = 0.05, after Levene’s check (p = 0.4485).

Objective 1 · Task 2

Match-day attendance

Is average stadium attendance significantly different between knockout and group-stage matches?

1

Wrangle

All 104 official matches carry a reported attendance. A binary knockout flag splits Group Stage from Round of 32 through the Final.

2

Sample

Stratified equal allocation: the full knockout census (n = 32) plus a simple random sample of 32 of 72 group matches.

3

Test

H₀: μknockout = μgroup. Two-sample t-test at α = 0.05 (Levene p = 0.9385).

Objective 1 · Task 3

Squad market value

Do UEFA squads carry a significantly higher market value than non-UEFA squads?

1

Wrangle

One Transfermarkt-valued 26-man squad per nation. Split the 48 teams into UEFA versus everyone else (CONMEBOL, CONCACAF, CAF, AFC, OFC).

2

Sample

Population: 16 UEFA / 32 non-UEFA. Balanced simple random sample of n = 14 per group.

3

Test

H₀: μUEFA = μnon-UEFA against H₁: μUEFA > μnon-UEFA. One-sided two-sample t-test.

Caveats

Limitations

i

Sampling on purpose

Objective 1 draws samples rather than using the full compiled population, to demonstrate a sampling technique and keep the two t-test groups balanced.

ii

Snapshot values

Transfermarkt squad values and average ages move a few percent with the pull date. Rankings, titles, scores, and attendance are unambiguous facts.

iii

Count-valued goals

OLS is used for interpretability on the team-goals model. First-match rest days are filled with the tournament-median rest — a neutral, pre-tournament baseline.
